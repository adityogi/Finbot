Tips for Setting Financial Goals

  • Be Specific: Define exactly what you want to achieve.
  • Make it Measurable: Set concrete amounts and deadlines.
  • Be Realistic: Set ambitious but achievable goals.
  • Prioritize: Focus on high-impact goals first.
  • Review Regularly: Track your progress and adjust as needed.

Common Financial Goals:

  • Debt Repayment

  • Education Fund
